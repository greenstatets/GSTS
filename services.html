Capability Set

Six service lines. One owner on every job.

Each line below is scoped by size, access, and hazard level — the same three factors driving the online estimate tool.

Tree Removal

Complete removals from open-yard trees to critical, tight-space work against structures, power lines, and fences. Rigging and crane options for anything that can't be dropped clean.

Tree Pruning

Crown thinning, deadwood removal, and shaping to keep a tree healthy and clear of structures. Priced to tree height, not guesswork.

Storm Risk Assessment

An on-site inspection of lean, root heave, deadwood, and canopy load — before a storm puts it to the test, or right after one already has. Bundled with your free estimate, not billed separately.

Removal & Replant Permits

Portland and surrounding jurisdictions require permits for many removals and mandate replanting. We file the paperwork and handle inspection scheduling.

Storm Cleanup

Downed limbs, split trunks, and blocked driveways cleared fast. Billed by the hour on-site since every storm leaves a different mess.
